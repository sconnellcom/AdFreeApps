What is Times Tables?

Times Tables is a simple practice app for multiplication facts from 2 × 2 through 12 × 12. Flip each square flash card, decide whether you got it correct, and gradually uncover a hidden image as you work through every combination.

Features

  • 🎲 Random flash cards for every problem from 2 × 2 through 12 × 12, with mastered facts skipped when you come back later
  • 🃏 Clean square cards whose front shows only the expression and whose back shows only the answer
  • ✅ Self-grade each card with “Got it right” or “Not yet,” plus an undo button if you tap the wrong one
  • 🧩 Hidden-image progression with a settings option to count progress by solid-or-better speed or by any correct answer
  • 🖼️ Use the built-in image or paste your own image URLs; changing the image list starts a fresh covered puzzle
  • 🟨 Optional pick-a-card mode lets you tap image squares to choose the next prompt instead of getting the next card automatically
  • 🔗 Share links can include your image URL list and current checkbox settings so someone else can open the same setup
  • 📊 Separate Flash Cards, Stats, and Settings screens keep your progress, timing categories, current image progress, and session totals easy to revisit
  • 💾 Progress, stats, settings, and your optional image URL list are saved in your browser when storage is available

How sessions work

Timing is measured from when a card appears until you flip it, with long idle gaps capped so walk-away time does not count. Correct answers are grouped into Mastered, Automatic, Solid, Learning, and Distracted buckets on the stats screen. Incorrect answers count toward your session totals but are not included in the timing buckets.

Newly Mastered counts how many multiplication facts became mastered during the current session. A fact becomes mastered after two recent correct answers with a fast enough average response time, even if you are using the easier progress mode for image reveals.

Privacy

Your progress and optional image URL list stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server, and the app includes no ads or trackers.
